# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

A visual editor for the supplied conversation workflow assessment. Users create, connect, move, edit, and delete nodes through a canvas and URL-addressable details drawers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Vue Flow, Vue Router, Pinia, TanStack Query, and shadcn-vue. The supplied JSON is bundled as the assessment sample and fetched on first use. Changes and attachments persist locally in the browser. Business hours use weekly times and a timezone. Success and failure markers are display-only. Edits use Save and Cancel; deletion retains downstream nodes.

## Brand Commitments

Follow the supplied light dotted-canvas mockup with compact cards and a right details drawer. Build directly in code.
